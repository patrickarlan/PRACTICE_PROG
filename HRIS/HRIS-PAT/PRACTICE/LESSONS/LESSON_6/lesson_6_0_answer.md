### Task 3: Compare Model vs DTO
*Compare `backend/Models/` vs `backend/DTOs/`:*
- **Model Chosen:** AccomplishmentReports.cs 
- **DTO Chosen:** AccomplishmentReportCreateDto.cs
- **Fields left out of DTO:**   `ReportId`, `CreatedAt`, `ApprovedById`, `IsDeleted`
- **Why were they excluded?** 
  - `ReportId` is auto-generated by the database upon creation.
  - `CreatedAt` is stamped securely by the server (cannot trust the client's clock).
  - `ApprovedById` cannot be set by the creator for security (only an authorized reviewer can approve it later).
  - `IsDeleted` is an internal database flag that regular users shouldn't control during creation.
